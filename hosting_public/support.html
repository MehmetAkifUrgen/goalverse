Contact & Technical Support (İletişim)

For questions about gameplay, multiplayer rooms, Football SOS Pro subscriptions, or bug reports, please reach out to us:

  • Support Email: support@flutterfootbalquiz.firebaseapp.com
  • Response Time: Within 24–48 hours

Subscription Restoration & Account Deletion

  • Restore Purchases: Open Goalverse → Profile → Football SOS Pro → Tap Restore Purchases (Satın Alımları Geri Yükle).
  • Delete Account: Open Goalverse → Settings → Tap Delete Account (Hesabı Sil) to immediately remove your account and profile data.
