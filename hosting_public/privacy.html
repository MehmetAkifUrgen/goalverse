Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası — Goalverse: Football SOS

Effective Date / Yürürlük Tarihi: November 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect (Toplanan Veriler)

Goalverse: Football SOS collects only the data necessary to provide authentication, multiplayer matchmaking, leaderboards, and in-app purchase synchronization:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and authentication provider identifier (Firebase Authentication via Email/Password, Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, or Guest session).
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: ELO rating, match history, round scores, daily challenge progress, and unlocked cosmetics stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Subscriptions & Purchases: Server-verified purchase tokens and subscription entitlements (Football SOS Pro and Cosmetic Store items) processed via Apple App Store and Google Play Billing. We do not store credit card or payment instrument numbers.
  • Diagnostics & Advertising: Crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics and non-intrusive ads for free-tier users via Google AdMob.

2. How We Use Your Data (Verilerin Kullanımı)

We use your information strictly to synchronize your 1v1 multiplayer matches, maintain fair-play ELO leaderboards, restore your Football SOS Pro subscription and cosmetics across devices, and improve app stability. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Account & Data Deletion (Hesap ve Veri Silme)

You can permanently delete your account and all associated Firestore profile data at any time directly inside the app via Settings → Delete Account (Hesabı Sil), or by contacting our support team on the Support page.
